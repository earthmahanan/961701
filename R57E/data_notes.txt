Notes from IT
  - total_amount = quantity x unit_price - discount_amount
  - Walk-in (non-member) customers have an empty member_id and member_tier.
  - member_tier in transactions.csv is the tier recorded at the time of the
    transaction; tier in members.csv is the member's CURRENT tier.
  - Age is optional at sign-up. If a member skips it, the app stores 0.
  - The satisfaction survey on the receipt is optional:
    1 = very unsatisfied ... 5 = very satisfied.
