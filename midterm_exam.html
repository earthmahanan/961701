The case: Baan Kafe

Baan Kafe is a coffee chain with 8 branches — 6 in Bangkok and 2 in Chiang Mai. Eighteen months ago it launched a loyalty app with four tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum). Members receive discounts, and higher tiers receive discounts more often. Revenue has been flat for the last two quarters. The CEO has just hired you as the company's first data scientist.

This week you interviewed four people:

CEO (Khun Ploy): "Revenue is flat and I don't know why. I keep hearing that members stop coming after a few months. I want to understand what's going on and have a plan for next quarter. I have budget for ONE data project this quarter."

Head of Marketing (Khun Tee): "Our Gold and Platinum members are our best customers. If we give them bigger discounts, they'll come more often. I'd like the data to prove that so we can roll it out in November."

CFO (Khun Nok): "Discount costs went up a lot this year while revenue stayed flat. I suspect we're giving discounts to people who would buy anyway. I want discounts cut, not increased."

Head of Operations (Khun Beam): "Honestly, I don't trust our reports. One branch changed its POS system this year, and there are things in the data I can explain but nobody ever asks me. And the member data is messy — lots of people don't fill in their details."
